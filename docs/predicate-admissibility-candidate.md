## The seam

Capture-resistant aggregation must differentiate participants; privacy decides
*who learns* the discriminant; **neither answers who authored it.** A ZK proof
establishes *"this subject satisfies predicate P"* — it cannot establish *"P is a
witnessed, admissible discriminant fit for this use."* `signed ≠ witnessed`,
applied to predicates. The invariant: **no authority from predicate satisfaction
alone.**

## Wicket's role

Wicket is the **admissibility membrane** — the one place a predicate-witness is
admitted *for a specific claim/use*, or refused. Its refusal in this doctrine:

> **A declaration with no witness is inadmissible. A proof that a subject
> satisfies a predicate is not, by itself, grounds to admit the predicate.**

The chain Wicket gates: a predicate-*declaration* (authored/scoped/versioned) and
a predicate-*witness* (admissibility evidence) arrive; Wicket admits the witness
**for the exact stated use**, refusing `declaration ≠ witness` collapses and
refusing to treat proof-of-satisfaction as proof-of-admissibility. Admission is
the step where unwitnessed-but-cleanly-signed categories must be stopped —
precisely where privacy-preserving classification is most dangerous, because it
is most disguised.

Downstream: admission → Standing (authority) → LA (spend). Wicket owns the
`witness ≠ admission` link. Cite the AG canonical note for the full chain and
plane map. No Wicket change is implied; this is a handle for review.
